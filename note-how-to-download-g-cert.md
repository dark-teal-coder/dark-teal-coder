# How to Download G-Cert

1.	Open Outlook.
2.	Under the "Home" tab, click "CMMP Service Portal".
3.	Your CMMP profile page will open.
4.	Click "Account Certificate" on the left-hand menu.
5.	Click "Download" under g-Cert.
6.	You will receive an automated email from the CMMP Service Portal System containing the passcode to open the G-Cert file (the .pfx file).
